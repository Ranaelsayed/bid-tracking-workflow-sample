# Workflow

1. **Discover**: log the tender, source, deadline and estimated value.
2. **Go / no-go**: check fit, capacity, margin and risk. Record the decision and the reason.
3. **Prepare**: assign owners for technical, financial and legal sections. Set internal deadlines 3 days before the real one.
4. **Review**: one reviewer checks completeness against the requirements list.
5. **Submit**: confirm receipt and save the submitted package.
6. **Follow up**: note the result, request feedback, and record lessons learned.

## Go / no-go checklist
- [ ] Matches our services
- [ ] Deadline is realistic
- [ ] Required documents are available
- [ ] Expected margin is acceptable
